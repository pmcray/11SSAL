I had been pleased to find the letter from D{\'e}jeuner {\`a} Deux on the
doormat when I had got home a few nights before. I was pleased because
going out on a date was always fun. Scratch that. Going out with

out with them. I had been a member of D{\'a}D for nearly four months now and not all of the men I
had met had been borderline psychostalker serial killer candidates.
I had been on one or two entertaining dates, which were fine as far as they went, but there was no denying that I was in my mid-thirties now (just) and a women has

game. Although the probability of meeting The One (or The
Four-Fifths or The Two-Thirds) on any given day was very small, the
probability of not having met The One (etc.) after a hundred dates
was also very small, which meant that it was worth persevering through ninety-nine lousy dates. The mathematics
told me so. (The voice at the back of my head told me that this
might be one of the reasons why I never met The One (etc.). I
ignored it. One thing I did know was that numbers couldn't lie.)}

The good thing about D{\`a}D was that as a

perspective as I'd never got the hang of the half-hour lunch date at a Wendy's when I'd been a postdoc at
Stanford. I preferred my dates to have a slower pace than that, to

through telling you to go to Such-and-Such restaurant at a

profiles, no speculative emails disappearing into the no{\"o}sphere.
No leaving of awkward voicemail messages that were never returned. No

G{\'e}rard or Livebait, not restaurants I would choose to go to myself, but the sort of establishment that one might end up at on a not very exciting, but not disastrous first date (which meant middle-of-the-road, sort-of-all-right-because-you-knew-what-you-were-going-to-get kinds of dates).
Now and then, they sent even me to good restaurants like Skylon, places I would choose of my own volition, which at least meant that the date began on a positive note. All too often though, I found
myself being dispatched sent to the kind of restaurant that I would
never dream of visiting myself --- overpriced hotel restaurants, North African restaurants serving overcooked vegetables and undercooked lamb, the most egregious type
of Italian restaurant with fake Italian waiters and even faker
